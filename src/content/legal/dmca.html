2. Filing a Takedown Notice

If you believe content available on the Site infringes a copyright you own or control, please submit a written notice containing the following information:

  • A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or a person authorized to act on their behalf.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  • Identification of the material claimed to be infringing, with enough detail for us to locate it on the Site (e.g. a URL).
  • Your contact information, including address, telephone number, and email address.
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.
