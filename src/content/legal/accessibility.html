1. Our Commitment

BitsServer IT Lab is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities. We continually work to improve the user experience for everyone and apply relevant accessibility standards across bitsserver.com.

2. Conformance Standard

We aim to align the Site with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with disabilities and more usable for everyone in general.

3. Accessibility Features

  • Semantic HTML structure to support screen readers and assistive technologies.
  • Keyboard-navigable menus, forms, and interactive elements, including visible focus states.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful non-text content.
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and background across the dark theme.
  • Respect for the prefers-reduced-motion setting, which disables non-essential animation for visitors who request it.

4. Ongoing Efforts

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. As we add new features and content, including our growing library of free tools, we test for and address accessibility issues as part of our regular development process.

5. Known Limitations

Despite our efforts, some parts of the Site may not yet be fully accessible, particularly interactive 3D or animated elements. We are working to address these limitations and welcome feedback to help us prioritize improvements.
