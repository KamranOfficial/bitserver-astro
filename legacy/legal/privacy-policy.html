1. Introduction

BitsServer IT Lab ("BitsServer", "we", "us", or "our") provides AI automation, cloud infrastructure, VPS hosting, web and mobile development, cybersecurity, and IT consulting services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through bitsserver.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Contact details submitted through our contact form or email, such as name, email address, phone number, company name, and the content of your message.
  • Information you provide when requesting a quote, onboarding for a service, or communicating with our support team.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data such as IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, pages visited, and time spent on the Site.
  • Cookies and similar technologies, described in detail in our Cookie Policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries, quote requests, and support tickets.
  • To deliver, maintain, and improve our services and this Site.
  • To send service-related communications and, where you have opted in, occasional updates about new tools or offerings.
  • To monitor for fraud, abuse, and security incidents affecting our infrastructure.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations in Pakistan, the United Arab Emirates, and other jurisdictions in which we operate.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable data protection law requires it, we process personal information based on your consent, the necessity of processing to perform a contract with you, our legitimate business interests in operating and securing the Site, or compliance with a legal obligation.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Trusted service providers who support hosting, analytics, email delivery, or payment processing on our behalf, under confidentiality obligations.

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, comply with our legal and contractual obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Because BitsServer operates from Multan, Pakistan and Abu Dhabi, UAE, your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We take reasonable steps to ensure such transfers comply with applicable data protection requirements.
