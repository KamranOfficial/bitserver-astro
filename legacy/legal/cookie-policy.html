1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function correctly, remember your preferences, and provide insight into how visitors use the site. Similar technologies covered by this Policy include local storage and pixel tags.

2. How BitsServer Uses Cookies

CategoryPurpose
Strictly NecessaryEnable core site functionality such as navigation, security, and load balancing. The Site cannot function properly without these.
Performance & AnalyticsHelp us understand how visitors interact with the Site so we can improve content, layout, and page speed.
FunctionalRemember choices you make, such as display or region preferences, to provide a more personalized experience.
MarketingUsed, where enabled, to measure the effectiveness of our outreach and tailor relevant messaging.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be set by trusted third-party services we use for analytics or embedded content. These providers have their own privacy and cookie practices, which we encourage you to review.

4. Managing Cookies

Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies through their settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect the functionality of the Site. You can typically manage cookie preferences via your browser's "Settings" or "Privacy" menu.

5. Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" signal. Because there is no common industry standard for responding to such signals, our Site does not currently alter its behavior when one is detected.
